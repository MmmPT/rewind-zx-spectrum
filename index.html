Getting started

Ready to rewind?

  1. Select a ZX Spectrum 128K PAL model in your emulator.
  2. Load the TAP using LOAD "", or open the SNA snapshot directly.
  3. Press Space after the loading screen, then choose 1 for Normal or 2 for Full.

The menu is silent. Music starts with the quiz.
